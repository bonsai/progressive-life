# Relation

Relation is the core value layer of progressive-life.

## Principle

**つなげることができるほど、半生記は豊かになる。**

A Relation connects two Episodes, Events, Decisions, or other entities.

## Lifecycle

Candidate → Human Confirmation → Confirmed Relation

## AI

AI may discover possible relationships from existing records.

Examples:

- influenced
- caused
- repeated
- contrasted_with
- led_to
- connected_to
- remembered_with

These are hypotheses until confirmed by the person.

## History

A confirmed Relation becomes part of the historical model and can affect future Views.
