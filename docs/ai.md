# AI

AI is a discovery partner, not the author of the person's life.

## Responsibilities

- Transcribe Voice
- Extract Episode candidates
- Extract Event candidates
- Find possible Relations
- Explain why a Relation may exist
- Ask for confirmation
- Suggest what to talk about next

## Boundary

AI must distinguish:

- observed / spoken
- inferred
- candidate
- human-confirmed

AI must not silently convert inference into historical fact.

## Core Loop

History → Latest → Now → Action

The next Action may be another question, another Voice recording, or confirmation of a Relation.
