# Episode

An Episode is a meaningful fragment of a life story.

## Purpose

Episode is the unit that can later be connected to other memories.

## Characteristics

- It may be chronological or non-chronological.
- It may have an uncertain date.
- It may contain multiple Events.
- It may be connected to many other Episodes.
- It remains linked to its source Voice.

## Field

The Episode Field should allow episodes to be freely positioned and brought closer together to discover relationships.

The system should not force a single timeline.
