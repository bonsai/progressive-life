# Voice

Voice is the source of the half-life story.

## Principle

**半生記を語る。**

The user does not need to prepare a complete autobiography before recording. A memory, event, person, place, decision, feeling, or question can become a Voice fragment.

## Rules

- Original audio is immutable.
- Each recording receives a stable reference.
- Transcript is derived data.
- Episodes may be extracted from one Voice.
- One Voice may contain multiple Episodes.
- Exact dates are optional.

Voice is the closest representation of what was actually said.
